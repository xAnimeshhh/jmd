Sound & Notifications

Alerts for due reminders & upcoming festivals
Plays a sound + shows a notification when a Fixed Payment is due/overdue. For festivals, it also speaks it out loud in Hindi (e.g. "Ganesh Chaturthi 12 din mein hai") once it enters its 15-day countdown. This is a per-device setting.

Sale Formula

Total Sale = Cash Taken Home + Counter Cash Kept + GPay/Online + Counter Expenses − Yesterday's Counter Cash
Example: Took ₹2000 home, left ₹500 in counter, received ₹1000 GPay, spent ₹200 from counter, yesterday's counter was ₹800 →
Sale = 2000 + 500 + 1000 + 200 − 800 = ₹2900
Profit = 40% × (Cash Taken Home + GPay/Online)
Counter Cash and Expenses are NOT included in profit calculation.
Profit = 40% × (2000 + 1000) = ₹1200
